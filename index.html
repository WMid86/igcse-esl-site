Welcome to the Reading Project

This reading project is all about helping you build the habit of sustained reading. By logging your reading minutes and sessions you create a record of your progress and provide data that helps us understand how well the programme is working. In turn this data enables teachers to identify strengths and areas for improvement and to adjust support accordingly. Reading for pleasure and fluency is the focus here, not test preparation, so we encourage you to choose books you enjoy and to track your reading honestly.

Students should log their reading minutes, sessions, books and reactions each week to see their own progress. Teachers will track participation across their classes and use your feedback to refine the project. Participating teachers include Mr Midlane, Mr Mcquay, Ms Liu, Mr Pottas, Mr Meyer and Ms Jenna.
